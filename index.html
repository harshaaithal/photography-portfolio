A little about me
H.

The photograph sets the direction.

I’m Harsha, drawn to the scale of a landscape, the shape of an object, and the feeling of moving through a place.

In Lightroom, I try to listen to each frame first. Its light, color, and mood guide the edit. I’m not looking for one preset to make every photograph feel the same.

This is a growing collection of work and a record of learning to see a little more clearly.

Thanks for looking.
